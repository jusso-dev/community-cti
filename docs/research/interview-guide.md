# Pilot interview guide

Draft for Justin. Not completed.

No interviews have been held. Do not treat this as findings.

## Purpose
Ask potential pilot organisations what would make a threat notice useful, and what consent they expect. This is a conversation guide, not a survey with results.

## Who to invite
Aboriginal Community Controlled Organisations, Land Councils, Aboriginal Medical Services and Indigenous corporations. Agree how people will be thanked for their time before anyone is contacted.

## Questions
1. Who looks after computers and email today?
2. What worried you the last time something odd happened online?
3. How would you want to hear about a problem: email, phone, or not at all?
4. Which domains and names are yours?
5. What should never be collected or shared?
6. Who is allowed to agree to monitoring?
7. What would you need to see before you trusted a warning?
8. Are there cultural protocols we must follow before we talk about incidents?

## Notes
Record notes only with consent, and store them outside this repository. A de-identified synthesis is still to be written after real conversations.
