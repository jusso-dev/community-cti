# Pilot interview guide

Status: draft. A community adviser has not reviewed this guide. No interviews have been held. No organisation has agreed to a pilot. Do not add a synthesis, a shortlist, or quotes from people.

Compensation and how participants are acknowledged are not agreed. Notes from any future interview stay out of this repository unless the person has consented and the notes are de-identified.

## Before you start
- Say who you are and that this is research, not a sale.
- Ask for consent to take notes. Stop if the answer is no.
- Offer to skip any question.
- Do not ask for passwords, customer records, or cultural stories that the person does not want recorded.

## Questions
1. What does the organisation do, and who looks after the computers?
2. What security help do you have today, if any?
3. Have you had a recent scare, such as a strange email, a fake website, or a locked computer? Share only what you are comfortable saying.
4. Which of those worries would you want to hear about first?
5. How would you like to be told? Email, a phone call, or something else? How often is too often?
6. Who should be allowed to see a finding? Who should be allowed to act on it?
7. What would you need to know before you let a service watch your domain names?
8. What should never be shared with other organisations, or with a sponsor?
9. Are there cultural protocols we should follow before we visit, record, or talk about the organisation?
10. What would make a pilot useful, and what would make you stop?

## After the conversation
- Store notes outside this repository.
- Do not write names into `docs/research/` unless the person has agreed to be named.
- A synthesis file is not part of this draft. Create it only after real interviews, and keep it de-identified.
