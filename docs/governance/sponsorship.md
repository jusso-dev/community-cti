# Sponsorship principles

Status: draft. Justin Middler has not decided a pricing model. No pilot cost has been computed. The README prices and the ceilings in `config/tiers.ts` are separate hypotheses. Do not treat either table as a quote or as a decision.

## Principle
Sponsors can help pay for community access. Sponsors never get access to participating organisations' private intelligence.

That includes findings, evidence, credential exposure records, cultural data, audit history, and the fact that a particular organisation is a customer, unless that organisation has agreed to be named.

## What a sponsor may receive
- A public description of the program.
- Aggregate counts that do not identify an organisation, and only if the governance group later allows that reporting.
- Recognition that the sponsor funds access, worded so it does not imply they see the data.

## What a sponsor does not receive
- A login to another organisation's tenant.
- Exports, raw feeds, or model prompts built from tenant data.
- A veto on an organisation's consent or sharing choices.
