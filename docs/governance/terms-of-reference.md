# Community governance group: terms of reference

Status: DRAFT. The group has not met. Membership is not agreed. This draft does not endorse `docs/DATA-GOVERNANCE.md`, the cultural data definition, or the sharing rules. Sharing stays off until that endorsement exists. See issue 42 and issue 43.

## Purpose
Review how participating organisations' data is collected, kept, and shared. The group exists so communities decide, rather than the builder deciding alone.

## Membership
- Representatives invited from pilot organisations once those organisations exist.
- A chair chosen by the members, not by a sponsor.
- Room for an adviser the members ask for.
- No sponsor seat with access to another organisation's findings.

Names are not listed here because no one has been invited.

## Meetings
- Cadence to be set by the first members. A placeholder of four times a year is not a decision.
- Notes record decisions, dissent, and who was in the room.
- Decisions go in `docs/governance/decisions.md` only after the group actually makes them. That file is not created yet.

## Decision making
- Aim for consensus of the organisation representatives.
- A sponsor, a vendor, or a staff member who does not represent a participant does not outvote the representatives.
- Cultural data rules need the agreement described in `docs/DATA-GOVERNANCE.md` before any exception.

## Conflicts
- Members say when a decision affects their own organisation, employer, or family.
- They may stay in the discussion. They do not silently approve a rule that only benefits them.

## Remuneration
Not agreed. Interview time and meeting time should be paid or otherwise recognised. The amount is a decision for the group and for Justin Middler, and it is not set in this draft.

## What this draft does not do
- It does not create a legal entity.
- It does not amend the data governance document.
- It does not turn sharing on.
